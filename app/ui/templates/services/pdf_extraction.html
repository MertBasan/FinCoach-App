What it does

Upload a bank statement PDF. FinCoach detects the bank, parses every transaction, verifies the balance chain mathematically, and gives you back a clean Excel file.

Supported banks

  • Halkbank (vector PDFs, both layouts)
  • Akbank
  • Ziraat (vector + scanned/OCR)
  • Yapı Kredi
  • Kuveyt Türk
  • Türkiye Finans Katılım
  • Other banks: generic fallback parser
